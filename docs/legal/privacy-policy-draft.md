This policy covers the Gameroom games, including **Sawmill Tycoon**, on the web and in the Android app.

## What we collect

- **No account, no sign-in.** We do not ask for your name, e-mail or other personal details.
- **Game progress** is saved only on your device (browser storage or app storage). Deleting the app or clearing site data removes it.
- **Anonymous usage statistics** (for example: a game was started, a level or upgrade was reached, a session length). They use a random identifier created on your device, not linked to you. They help us improve the games. _(Only if analytics is enabled; provider: [PostHog, EU hosting].)_

## Advertising

- The Android app shows ads from **Google AdMob**. You choose to watch "rewarded" ads (for example, double income for 3 minutes); short full-screen ads may appear only at natural breaks.
- AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and device information to show and measure ads. In the EU/EEA/UK you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later.
- See how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Games published on web portals (e.g. CrazyGames, Poki) show ads through that portal's SDK under the portal's own privacy policy.

## Children

Sawmill Tycoon is not directed at children under 13. Games marked for children use non-personalised ads only and collect no personal data.
